DAYBREAK

DAYBREAK is a swarm of solar collectors surrounding the Sun. It was built to:

  • collect energy from the Sun,
  • observe the Sun continuously,
  • gather extremely precise solar data, and
  • transmit that data back to humanity.

It is, by any measure, the greatest thing we have built.
